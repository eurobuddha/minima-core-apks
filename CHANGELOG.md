- 2026-07-05 · minimaSwap 0.14.5 — Ladder now advertises by TOTAL balance (multi-UTXO), not single coins. The published ask ladder was clamped to how many single coins were >= the tranche size, hiding tranches the responder could actually fill by combining coins (0.14.3). Now it advertises every ask tranche whose cumulative amount your total free MINIMA covers — e.g. 18.98k backs all 6 of a 6x1000 ladder. Applies to the taker's propose/counter and the LP's accept. The on-chain lock stays the backstop; this is a fast pre-check from cached balances.
- 2026-07-05 · minimaSwap 0.14.3 — Fix swaps/OTC hanging on any deal larger than a single coin. The MINIMA counter-leg lock now combines MULTIPLE UTXOs (like a wallet send) up to your total balance, instead of requiring one coin >= the deal size — so a fragmented wallet (many small coins) can fill large deals (e.g. 3000+). New lockFromCoins does a multi-input lock with change back; greedy largest-first selection minimizes inputs; concurrent burst locks still never double-select a coin. If the total genuinely can't cover the deal it now shows a clear 'need X, only Y free / consolidate' notice instead of hanging silently. Fixes BOTH the ladder BUY responder and the OTC BUY responder. Root cause of the 3000-MINIMA OTC hangs.
- 2026-07-05 · minimaSwap 0.14.2 — Fix OTC BUY deals hanging (never completing). Root cause: the deal hashlock was stored Minima-UPPERCASE but looked up via the web3j-lowercase on-chain hashlock with an exact match, so the LP's responder never found the deal. OtcDb now normalizes the hash (case/0x-insensitive, matching SwapDb), which also lets an in-flight stuck deal recover. Plus: re-arm the buy responder from persisted EXECUTING deals so a fg/bg engine handoff or restart can't strand it; resend a lost EXECUTE (throttled, into the timelock window); and stop a too-early PROPOSE from self-dropping its dedup slot. Reviewed across 3 passes; BUY+SELL now complete end-to-end.
